Lupus Gene Explorer

Genes ranked by systemic lupus erythematosus literature, updated from PubMed, PubTator 3 and Open Targets

Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE, or lupus) is a chronic autoimmune disease in which the immune system attacks the body's own tissues — most often the skin, joints, kidneys, and blood cells. Its causes span hundreds of genes involved in interferon signaling, immune-complex clearance, and B-cell regulation. This tracker follows that genetic landscape as the research literature evolves.
